About Us

Small local business founded in 2022 in Magxaki, Port Elizabeth, by a qualified 23-year-old nail technician, Noma’s BeautyParlour began as a dedicated nail care studio. Driven by client trust and an increasing demand for specialized beauty treatments, the parlor expanded into professional pedicure care, custom lash extensions, and flawless hair installations.

Our Mission

At Noma's Beauty Parlour ,our mission is to uplift day to day beauty by delivering high-quality ,long lasting nail care ,clean and neat hair installations,and custom lash enhancements in a welcoming hygienic ,and relaxing environment. We are dedicated to honoring customers individually by detail orientated artistry and reliable client care right in the heart of the community

Our Vision

To become the premier local salon known for trendsetting beauty aesthetics and seamless digital client care.
